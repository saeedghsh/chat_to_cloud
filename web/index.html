GETTING YOUR CHAT

How to export

In WhatsApp, open the chat, then choose More → Export chat → Without media, and save the .txt file. If you get a .zip, unzip it first.

Exports from Android, with lines like 7/4/19, 15:50 - Alice: Hello, are supported.

Animations are rendered frame by frame on your device and take from about a minute to much longer for big chats.
